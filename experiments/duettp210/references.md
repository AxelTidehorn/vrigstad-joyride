# Reference scope — Duett P210 EPA pickup

Research and visual inspection: 15 November 2026. No AI reference images, third-party vehicle meshes or earlier vehicle bodies were used.

## Primary conversion: one actual vehicle

* [Liftarn, Duett-EPA.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Duett-EPA.jpg), photographed in Hova, Sweden, 2006: front/near-side three-quarter.
* [Liftarn, Duett-EPA-rear.jpg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Duett-EPA-rear.jpg), same vehicle, rear/near-side three-quarter.

These two photographs define the conversion: retained long crowned bonnet, strongly rolled wings, short two-door cab, upright cut rear wall, open short bed and exposed traditional rear mudguards. The model interprets this one build, with pale paint and simple stock wheels instead of copying its wear/roof visor. No exact registration, construction year, legal EPA specification or conversion dimensions are certified from these photographs. In particular, these views have perspective and are not exact orthographic side drawings.

## Original shell and dimensional anchor

[Volvo Cars P210 Duett history](https://www.volvocars.com/intl/cars/legacy-models/p210-duett/): manufacturer explicitly states **wheelbase 2600 mm**, production 1960–1969, adoption of a curved windscreen in autumn 1960. This establishes the P210 curved, unsplit windshield rather than the earlier PV445 split-screen front. No drivetrain specification from that page was implemented.

Supplemental original vehicle: [1966 P210 Duett B18, Bilweb Auctions](https://www.bilwebauctions.se/juliauktion-3-2026/volvo-p210-duett-b18-55071).

Supplemental conversion observations (not alternate proportions): [Duett a-traktor 1](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Duett_a-traktor_1.jpg) and [Duett a-traktor 2](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Duett_a-traktor_2.jpg), Fmmattsson, 2014: upright screen, simple front/grille, cab rear glazing and open bed. The modern wheels and painted decoration are excluded. A Bilweb 1962 Grip coachbuilt pickup was investigated but rejected as primary because it is not the selected EPA conversion.

Photographs were downloaded only for inspection outside the repository and are linked here, not redistributed in the vehicle package. Attribution/licence metadata can be retrieved from their Commons file pages. The new vehicle mesh and review renders are original project assets under this repository's licence.

## Dimensions and uncertainty

| Quantity | Model | Evidence |
|---|---:|---|
| Wheelbase | 2600 mm | Manufacturer verified |
| Front/rear axle Y | +1100 / -1500 mm | Coordinate placement, exact 2600 separation |
| Overall length including bumpers | 4365 mm | Model measurement; EPA interpretation, not verified factory overall length |
| Main wing width | 1660 mm | Model measurement / visual estimate |
| Maximum height | 1700 mm | Model measurement / visual estimate |
| Track | 1300 mm | Estimated original-style stance |
| Tyre diameter × width | 670 × 170 mm | Estimated stock-style proportions |
| Bed interior L × W | 1530 × 1140 mm | Conversion estimate |
| Bed floor / top / depth | 760 / 1080 / 320 mm | Conversion estimate, positive wheel clearance |
| Cab rear / cowl longitudinal positions | approx -480 / +600 mm | Estimated from primary conversion |

Total length alone was not used to approve the shape. Exact orthographic Blender side was compared manually against the two perspective Hova photographs: wheel centres, hood/cowl, cab height/length, screen slope, cut rear wall, rear axle and bed boundaries. Perspective prevents defensible millimetre silhouette errors or a valid full-mask IoU. No automated photographic silhouette score is claimed.
