# XC70 P2 MY2005: reference register

Selected body: facelift P2 XC70, approximately MY2005–2006, European civilian
silver/charcoal trim. Police: current user-requested blue/white scheme; earlier blue/yellow references retained as historical research only.
Not P3 and not the unclad V70. Photographed model years are not always exact.

- Volvo Cars, 2005 Swedish press information:
  https://www.volvocars.com/se/media/press-releases/6AC9A76BA60CE762/
  Corroborates length 4733 mm and wheelbase 2763 mm.
- Volvo UK XC70 technical specification, April 2003:
  https://www.volvoclub.org.uk/tech/XC70_Tech_Spec_Apr2003.pdf
  Length 4733, width excluding mirrors 1860, unladen height 1562, wheelbase 2763,
  front track 1611, rear track 1548, ground clearance 209 mm. The side photograph
  and rear image are real manufacturer reference views. `side.png` and `rear.png`
  are cropped/resized technical-study excerpts from page 1. Original © Volvo;
  no game-asset reuse licence claimed. Side image has perspective and is
  pre-facelift: useful for architecture, not exact MY2005 fascia or mm approval.
  Side crop from PDF raster width 2000: (190,460,880,710), resized 1380×500.
- Bull-Doser, 2005–2007 facelift XC70 front three-quarter photographs:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:'05-'07_Volvo_XC70_2.5T_AWD.JPG
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:'05-'07_Volvo_XC70_Cross_Country.JPG
  Public-domain dedication by photographer. `front.jpg`, `front-2.jpg` unchanged;
  `front-detail.jpg` is a resized crop. These show front lamps, hood crown,
  grille, wheel arch trim, windscreen and pillars. Roof box is not modelled.
- Ethan Llamas, 2006 XC70 rear-right photograph, 13 July 2024:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2006_Volvo_XC70_Cross_Country_2.5T_AWD_in_Lunar_Gold_Metallic,_rear_right,_07-13-2024.jpg
  CC BY-SA 4.0 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ .
  `rear-three-quarter.jpg` is resized to max 1600px. Used for tall lamps,
  tailgate and rear cladding. Non-European plate/market details not copied.
- Fifo, Swedish police XC70, photograph April 2006:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Polisbil_Volvo_V70.jpeg
  CC BY-SA 2.5 https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/2.5/ .
  `police.jpg` unchanged; documents the older blue/white scheme used for the current
  user-requested treatment. Later blue/yellow photos below are historical research. Confirms period raised blue bar/fitment.
- Per Enström / Plrk, P2 Swedish police XC70 (caption MY2007), 30 April 2009:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Swedish_police_car_(Volvo_XC70)_outside_Ekonomikum,_Uppsala,_2009-04-30.jpg

- Scheme introduction documented in Commons description of
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Swedish_patrol_car_new_livery.JPG
  (National Police Board decision 9 May 2005). Used for chronology only.

Uncertainties: no exact orthographic MY2005 front photo was obtained; front is
checked against two real three-quarter views, rear against near-straight and
three-quarter views. Wheel size .690 m is an estimate consistent with 215/65R16,
not measured from the perspective photos. Both variants deliberately share axle
positions (front/rear lateral track simplified to 1611 mm rather than separate
rear 1548 mm). Steel shell width is 1800 mm; cladding extends to documented
1860 mm. The silhouette mask excludes rails, mirrors, shadow and arrows;
reference bumpers and model hull envelopes differ. Reports never certify exact
millimetre shape errors. No AI-generated reference imagery.
