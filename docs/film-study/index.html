Vrigstad Joyride · photographic color study

Four photographic interpretations in the actual playable game. These are not exact reproductions of proprietary film simulations. Classic Negative remains the default. Open any image for its full 1280 × 720 capture.

All looks at each location share one paused world, camera, exposure and static gameplay lighting (lighting=off). Grain and mist are disabled in the primary comparison. DOM HUD and map remain visible and ungraded; they still show the player at Torget while the review camera visits other locations. Camera coordinates and state are recorded in capture results.
